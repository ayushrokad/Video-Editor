menu_book FRAMEFLOW Workspace & Docking Guide

Customizable Panel Docking: Drag any panel tab to the center of another panel to group it as a tab, or drop on the Left, Right, Top, or Bottom edge to create a new split dock.

Move Entire Groups: Drag the 6-dot handle on the left of a tab bar to relocate or merge an entire panel group at once.

Stacked Panel Groups: Right-click any panel tab (or click ⋮) and choose Switch Group to Stacked Mode. Enable Solo Mode to keep only one stacked panel expanded at a time.

Floating & Redocking: Click the undock icon (or Alt+Drag a tab) to float a panel inside the viewport. Drag a floating header to any dock edge or click the Redock button to return it.

Maximize & Restore: Double-click any panel tab or press ` (backtick) to maximize/restore the active dock group.

Non-Destructive Workspaces: Switch workspaces freely (or drag workspace tabs to reorder them) — your selected clips, playhead position, timeline zoom, and active playback are always preserved.
