## Trimming Mechanics
- **Ripple Trim (`Q` / `W`):** Ripples head or tail of the clip to the playhead on targeted tracks.
- **Roll Trim (`N`):** Moves the boundary between two adjacent clips without changing sequence duration.
- **Slip (`Y`):** Adjusts clip In/Out points while keeping timeline position fixed.
- **Slide (`U`):** Moves clip along timeline while rippling adjacent clips.
- **Sub-frame Audio Trimming:** Nudging edit points in sub-frame sample increments to align with drum beats or dialogue plosives.
