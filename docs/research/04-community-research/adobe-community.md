# Community Research: Adobe Forums & Official Issue Trackers
See detailed synthesis in [user-pain-points.md](file:///h:/Ayush%20Work/Video%20Editor/docs/research/user-pain-points.md#category-b-automation-without-manual-precision-the-ai-black-box-trap).

## Key Takeaways
- Over-automation without manual fallback (e.g. AI auto-masking without bezier vertex editing) creates frustration.
- Selection of many layers and keyframes in complex compositions causes UI freezes.
- Users demand transparent performance telemetry.
