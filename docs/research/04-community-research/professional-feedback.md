## Core Feedback from Guild Editors
- Sub-frame audio trimming and waveform sync are essential for high-end dialogue editing.
- Standardized OTIO/XML interchange is non-negotiable for handoffs to color and sound.
- Deterministic undo/redo and autosave reliability are paramount.
