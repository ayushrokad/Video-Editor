## Panel Grouping & Geometry
- Docking regions: Left, Right, Center Top, Center Bottom, Floating.
- Tabbed panels inside groups allow high density without screen clutter.
- Contextual maximization (`~`) allows full-screen precision when working in the Timeline, Graph Editor, or Video Scopes.
